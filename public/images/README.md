# Projects page illustration

`contractor-documenting-job.png` was made for FieldProof with OpenAI's built-in
image generation tool on September 2, 2026 (Pacific time). It depicts a fictional
contractor and kitchen. It is decorative artwork, not a real job photo or evidence.
No customer photo or third-party reference image was supplied.

The production prompt used the generated page concept as its reference:

> Create only the standalone editorial gouache illustration shown on the right of
> the FieldProof hero. Remove all website interface and text. A carpenter viewed
> from behind in a navy shirt, tan work trousers and tool belt holds a smartphone
> horizontally to photograph fitted oak kitchen cabinets. Window on left,
> unfinished plaster and oak cabinets on right. Toolbox and tape measure on the
> workbench. Warm daylight, textured painted brushwork, deep navy, natural oak and
> amber. Match the reference pose, composition and style. Nearly square image,
> full canvas illustration, no borders, UI, text, logos or watermark.
